Before you begin

This is a student research project at UPES, Dehradun. We are studying whether the way a person types, and the words they use, can indicate their stress level.

What you will do

  • Four short blocks: copy a passage, then answer a few chat questions, first at your own pace and then against a timer.
  • After each block, rate how stressed you felt.

What gets recorded

  • The timing of each key you press and release, in milliseconds.
  • The text you type during the tasks on this page.
  • Your own rating of how stressed you feel after each block.

What does not get recorded

  • Nothing outside this page. This tool cannot see other tabs, applications, or anything you type elsewhere.
  • No name, email, or contact details. You are identified only by a code. Please do not type personal details in your answers.
  • Nothing is uploaded. Data stays in this browser until you download it and hand it to the research team.

Takes about 10–15 minutes. You can stop at any point by closing the tab, and nothing will be kept.
